# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF
## What is strong
- The one thing is clear and rare: a drive that people and AI agents share across machines. The Product lens asks for the experience first, and this has one: open Finder, the file is there, the agent on the VPS sees the same file.
- The proof is an experience, not a spec: three 4K/60fps renders on a 4-machine fleet in about 25 minutes versus an estimated 90+ serial, with the drive as queue, store and delivery. (Owner-run and the serial figure is an estimate; treat it as a lead.)
- Craft in unseen places: plain R2 objects, "Conflicted copy" instead of silent overwrite, evicted files return as placeholders. Billing is already built, so the ladder can be tested.
- The owner's margins reproduce for storage cost alone (e.g. Builder 256 GB at 60% use = $2.30, 87.9%).

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **The free tier hides the product.** Free has the MCP token, skill registry and fleet rendering off, so a free user gets a 10 GB BYOK mount that looks like Mountain Duck. The differentiator appears only at $19 (personal token) or $49 (fleet, skills). The best five minutes happens behind a paywall.
2. **Seven tiers and three limits is too much for a one-sentence product.** Builder to Creator and Studio to Studio Max differ only by quantity (storage, vaults, slots). A buyer cannot tell which limit they will hit. The 4-machine demo needs Studio, yet Creator has 4 slots and no fleet feature. Lifetime ($197, unlimited, fleet and skills included) beats Studio annual ($397) and is its own cheaper answer.
3. **Seams are untested, and copy outruns the product.** Free-to-hosted migration, Cloudflare account setup for free users, and the Worker serving an agency bucket at hosted speed are all unconfirmed. Lifetime copy names AWS and B2, but B2 has not passed a live test.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Say the product in one sentence. Which tier is that sentence?
- Count the steps from download to an agent reading a file, for a free user with no Cloudflare account. Has a non-owner done it unaided?
- Who buys Creator, and what do they do on day one that a Builder cannot?
- Why is Studio Max a tier and not storage added to Studio?
- Open: which tier do buyers pick (/founder-consumer)? What do request and Worker costs and Stripe fees do to margins, and what does Lifetime cost to support (/founder-cfo)? Are the competitor prices current (/founder-competitors)?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Launch with three paid tiers: Builder, Studio, Agency. Fold Creator into the gap between them and make Studio Max extra storage on Studio, as Agency already does at +$25 per TB. Do not build the $297 Agency BYOK until a real agency asks.
- Show buyers two limits, storage and machines. Drop vaults as a visible limit.
- Turn on the MCP token and skill registry in free, if /founder-cfo confirms the Worker cost is tolerable, so free users see an agent use the drive.
- Reprice or limit Lifetime so it cannot undercut Studio, and cut AWS and B2 from its copy until each passes a live test.
- Have three non-owners finish setup unaided before launch.
