# Offers lens
Score: 5 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The dream outcome is concrete: one vault that every machine and every agent sees, with no setup. The 2026-11-10 render test (about 25 minutes against an estimated 90+ serial) is a real result, but it is one test on the owner's own fleet.
- Studio ($49, 1 TB, unlimited slots, fleet rendering) holds up. Its features match the proof, and it sits below LucidLink's recorded $27-32 per seat plus about $80 per TB (unverified). It is priced on features, not only storage.
- Builder holds up as an entry tier. Zero-setup and a personal MCP token cut the buyer's effort. At 19/256 GB it is near Fabric Drive's recorded $18 for 250 GB, so it is not a price play.
- The owner's arithmetic checks out. Annual shares are right (97/228 = 43%, 4,970/5,964 = 83%). Monthly margins reproduce (Builder: 256 x 0.6 x 0.015 = $2.30, so 87.9%). 200 Agency x $4,970 = $994k. 1,700 Studio x $49 x 12 = $999.6k.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **BYOK Lifetime ($197 once) undercuts the ladder.** It includes fleet rendering, the skill registry, unlimited vaults and unlimited slots. That is every Studio feature, and $197 is under 4.1 months of Studio ($49) and half of Studio annual ($397). Any buyer who can make a bucket has no reason to pay monthly. It also adds support and Worker cost with no recurring revenue. Mountain Duck is $49 one-time (recorded). The copy names AWS and B2, but only R2 is decided and B2 has not passed a live test.
2. **The market and the buyer are untested, and the ladder is priced on gigabytes.** Storage is the commodity axis. The differentiator is agents and fleets, yet the free tier switches off MCP, the registry and fleet rendering, so a free user cannot try the hook. The proof case (4 machines plus fleet) needs Studio at $49 or more. Creator has 4 slots but no fleet. Nobody has bought anything. The likelihood part of the value equation is one owner test and no guarantee.
3. **Agency ($497) and the $1M claim rest on no demand evidence.** Agency is $0.0497 per GB, above Studio's $0.0479, so its value is white-label and sub-accounts only. Nothing shows that 200 agencies exist or want it. $297 Agency BYOK would add a second unproven tier.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who exactly has this pain and money today: video render teams, agent builders, agencies? How many can you reach, and where? (/founder-consumer, /founder-competitors)
- What do Builder buyers get that Fabric Drive does not? Why does Creator exist between $19 and $49? (/founder-pricing)
- What guarantee can you afford, and what do annual margins look like? On my arithmetic, Builder annual is about 71% and Studio Max annual about 63% (before Stripe fees and request costs). Which per-request costs apply at 797,000 objects per 148 GB? (/founder-cfo)
- Would a buyer double the price and still choose this? Which result would they pay for first? (/founder-consumer)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Cut or reprice BYOK Lifetime: remove fleet rendering and the registry, or price it at no less than 12 months of Studio, and drop AWS/B2 from the copy.
- Collapse to Free, Builder, Studio, Studio Max, and hold Creator and Agency until buyers ask for them.
- Let the free tier use a limited MCP token so the agent hook can be tried.
- Price and name tiers by outcome (machines, agents, jobs), not gigabytes, and add a guarantee the CFO has costed.
- Before building more, collect real pre-sales at Studio and Builder prices.
